Executive overview

Apollo carries the strongest budget-linked signal: its 3 November earnings call describes approximately $350m of planned expenses over two years for a broader private-markets technology and operating-model programme, including software retirement and sizable data investment. This is not a $350m AI procurement budget.

M&G is the strongest UK procurement touchpoint: a new data/AI hiring cluster includes a category manager explicitly responsible for market-data licensing, sourcing, vendor renewals and commercial negotiations. Cost optimisation, not net-new spend, is central to that mandate.

Investment-agent integration is becoming concrete: Carlyle and Blackstone vacancies explicitly reference MCP and agent data connectivity, while BlackRock/Preqin is staffing agentic private-markets intelligence. All are recruitment evidence, not confirmed external purchasing.

Test demand for provenance, private-company financials, entity hierarchies and credit data in agent interfaces.

Scope and evidence standard

Eight distinct underlying events dated 2–8 November 2026 are included. Previously reported late-October hires and recycled job listings are not counted again; a new hire within an established AI programme is characterised as incremental staffing, not a fresh funded programme. Recruitment sites can change posting timestamps and some employer Workday pages are inaccessible to public text extraction; limitations are explicitly stated. A vacancy is not proof of an RFP, supplier selection or additional external-data budget. No EMEA-specific new contract value was independently confirmed in this report.
